Fade — Help & support

A free game where friends post their own odds on real sports and fade each other's picks — with play-money coins only.

Fade coins are free play money. They can't be bought, sold, cashed out or redeemed for anything, and there are no prizes. Fade never handles real money.

Common questions

How do I join a group?

Ask a friend for their group's invite code (they find it on their Group screen → Share invite). In Fade tap + → Join with a code and type it in.

What is a "share" and how are odds set?

Each share pays 1 coin to whoever is right. You post an offer — the side you're backing, a price from 1¢ to 99¢, and a number of shares. If you back a team at 60¢, a friend who takes the other side pays 40¢ per share, and the winner collects 1 coin per share. You choose whether prices show as cents (60¢) or American odds (-150) in Settings.

What happens if a game is postponed or the result is disputed?

Bets always wait for the official result. If a result is disputed it can take several days; your coins stay safely set aside. If an event is cancelled and the result is a 50/50, everyone gets their own coins back.

I ran out of coins.

When you have no coins left (including coins in open offers and unsettled bets) you can buy back in, following your group's rule: unlimited, a limited number per week, or by group vote. Buybacks count against your net profit on the leaderboard.

How do I report or block someone?

Touch and hold a comment or a member's name and choose Report, Mute or Block. Blocked and muted people are listed in Settings, where you can undo it.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete my account…. It leaves your groups, voids and refunds unsettled bets for the other person, and removes your username, friends, comments and sign-in. This can't be undone.

If gambling is a problem for you

Even play-money betting can be hard to put down for some people. Free, confidential help is available 24/7 from the National Council on Problem Gambling (call or text 1-800-GAMBLER).

About the data

Game schedules and official results come from public third-party prediction-market data. Fade is not affiliated with or endorsed by any sports league, team, or data provider.
