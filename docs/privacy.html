Privacy policy

Effective October 2026. Fade is a free app run by [YOUR NAME] ("we", "us"). Contact: [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL].

The short version: we collect only what the game needs, we don't sell it, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites. You can delete your account and data inside the app at any time.

What we collect

  • Sign-in identifier. You sign in with Apple. We receive an identifier unique to our app. We do not ask for your name or email address.
  • Username. The name you choose. Other people in your groups see it.
  • Game activity. Groups you join, offers and bets you make, your coin balances, buybacks, votes, win–loss record and scores, friends and friend requests.
  • Things you write. Comments and emoji reactions.
  • Safety information. Reports you send, and people you block or mute.
  • Notification token. If you allow notifications, a token from Apple that lets us send them to your iPhone, and your notification preferences.
  • Technical logs. Our hosting provider records standard server logs (such as IP address and request time) for security and reliability.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone, health data, advertising identifier, or any payment information. There are no purchases in Fade.

How we use it

  • To run the game: show your groups, offers, bets, balances, leaderboards and history.
  • To send notifications you've turned on (new offers in your groups, your offer taken, a bet settled, votes).
  • To keep the community safe: review reports, remove harmful content, and disable abusive accounts.
  • To keep the service secure and working.

Who can see it

  • Members of your groups see your username, balance, record, bets, offers, comments and reactions in that group. Friends see your global score and record.
  • Our service providers process data for us: Supabase (database and sign-in hosting) and Apple (Sign in with Apple and push notifications). They may only use it to provide those services.
  • We read public game schedules and results from a third-party data source. No personal information is sent to it.
  • We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it to track you. We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect people's safety.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your account in the app: Settings → Delete my account. We then remove your username, friends, comments, reactions, notification token and sign-in, and anonymize your remaining history (for example, past results other members can still see show "deleted user"). Reports that were sent or made about you are kept for safety reasons, without your username. Group records have no cash value; they are kept only so other members' histories stay accurate. Server logs are kept for a short period (typically up to 30 days).

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with access controls that restrict each person to their own groups' data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

Fade has the highest age rating (17+/18+) because it simulates betting with play money. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has used Fade, contact us and we will delete the account.

Your choices and rights

You can change your username, turn notifications on or off, block or mute people, and delete your account in the app. If you'd like a copy of your data or have a privacy question, email us. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights under local law (for example, to access, correct or delete your data); we will honor valid requests.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way we will update the date above and, where appropriate, tell you in the app.
